# Native Title After *Mabo*

The High Court rejected the doctrine of terra nullius.[^1] Brennan J described the common law as 'frozen in an age of racial discrimination'.[^2] The statutory scheme followed soon after.[^3]

Later cases considered extinguishment,[^4] and the role of the *Native Title Act* has been widely debated.[^5]
